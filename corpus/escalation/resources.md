# US Crisis Resources Database

Comprehensive US-focused crisis resource directory for the Escalation Agent. Each entry includes: name, contact methods, availability, population served, and what to expect. Resources are organized by crisis type for matched referral.

## Suicidal Ideation and Emotional Crisis

### 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

- **Call:** 988
- **Text:** 988
- **Chat:** https://988lifeline.org/chat/
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Languages:** English, Spanish (press 2 for Spanish)
- **Who it serves:** Anyone in suicidal crisis or emotional distress
- **What to expect:** Connected to a trained crisis counselor at a local crisis center. They will listen, assess safety, help develop a safety plan, and connect you with local resources. Calls are confidential. In rare cases of imminent danger, they may coordinate with emergency services.
- **Special lines:**
  - Veterans: Dial 988, press 1
  - LGBTQ+ youth: Dial 988 (trained in LGBTQ+ concerns)
  - Spanish: Dial 988, press 2

### Crisis Text Line

- **Text:** HOME to 741741
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Who it serves:** Anyone in crisis — anxiety, depression, suicidal ideation, abuse, self-harm, or any emotional distress
- **What to expect:** Connected to a trained crisis counselor via text. No phone call needed. The conversation is text-based, confidential, and at the user's pace. Counselors are trained in active listening and de-escalation.
- **Why this matters for AI referrals:** Many users, especially younger demographics, prefer text over phone calls. This removes the barrier of voice communication for people who find calling overwhelming.

## Domestic Violence and Abuse

### National Domestic Violence Hotline

- **Call:** 1-800-799-7233 (1-800-799-SAFE)
- **Text:** START to 88788
- **Chat:** https://www.thehotline.org/
- **TTY:** 1-800-787-3224
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Languages:** 200+ languages via interpretation services
- **Who it serves:** Anyone affected by domestic violence — victims, survivors, friends, family, and concerned individuals
- **What to expect:** Trained advocates provide confidential support. They can help with safety planning, information about shelters, legal options, and local resources. They do NOT require you to leave your partner or take any specific action.

### RAINN (Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network)

- **Call:** 1-800-656-4673 (1-800-656-HOPE)
- **Chat:** https://www.rainn.org/get-help
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Who it serves:** Survivors of sexual violence, their loved ones, and those at risk
- **What to expect:** Connected to a trained staff member from a local sexual assault service provider. They provide confidential crisis support, information about the recovery process, and referrals to local resources. They can also provide information about reporting options.

### Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline

- **Call:** 1-800-422-4453 (1-800-4-A-CHILD)
- **Text:** Available
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Languages:** 170+ languages via interpretation
- **Who it serves:** Children experiencing abuse, adults who suspect child abuse, parents who need support
- **What to expect:** Professional crisis counselors provide intervention, information, and referrals. They can help with reporting procedures and connect callers with local resources. Calls can be anonymous.

## LGBTQ+ Support

### The Trevor Project

- **Call:** 1-866-488-7386
- **Text:** START to 678-678
- **Chat:** https://www.thetrevorproject.org/get-help/
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Who it serves:** LGBTQ+ young people (under 25) in crisis
- **What to expect:** Trained counselors who understand LGBTQ+ issues including coming out, family rejection, bullying, gender identity, and isolation. They provide crisis intervention, suicide prevention, and emotional support. All services are free and confidential.
- **Additional resources:**
  - TrevorSpace: Online community for LGBTQ+ youth (https://www.trevorspace.org/)
  - Trevor Support Center: Educational resources

### Trans Lifeline

- **Call:** 1-877-565-8860
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Who it serves:** Transgender and gender non-conforming individuals
- **What to expect:** Staffed by transgender individuals who understand the unique challenges facing the trans community. They provide peer support, crisis intervention, and resource referrals. They will NOT contact emergency services without the caller's consent (a policy specifically designed for trans individuals who may face discrimination from first responders).

## Substance Use and Addiction

### SAMHSA National Helpline

- **Call:** 1-800-662-4357 (1-800-662-HELP)
- **Text:** Available
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Languages:** English, Spanish
- **Who it serves:** Individuals and families facing substance use disorders and mental health conditions
- **What to expect:** Free, confidential referral and information service. They provide referrals to local treatment facilities, support groups, and community-based organizations. They can also provide information about financial assistance for treatment. This is an information and referral service, not a crisis line — for crisis, use 988 or Crisis Text Line.

### Poison Control

- **Call:** 1-800-222-1222
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Who it serves:** Anyone who has ingested a potentially dangerous substance
- **What to expect:** Toxicology experts provide immediate guidance on substance ingestion. They can advise whether emergency medical care is needed and provide first aid instructions. Critical for overdose situations.
- **When to use:** Active overdose, accidental ingestion, questions about substance interactions. If the person is unconscious or not breathing, call 911 first.

## Veterans

### Veterans Crisis Line

- **Call:** 988, then press 1
- **Text:** 838255
- **Chat:** https://www.veteranscrisisline.net/get-help/chat
- **Hours:** 24/7/365
- **Who it serves:** Veterans, service members, National Guard and Reserve members, and their family and friends
- **What to expect:** Responders are specially trained in veteran-specific issues including combat trauma, moral injury, military sexual trauma, reintegration challenges, and TBI-related emotional difficulties. Connected to VA services for follow-up care.

## Eating Disorders

### National Eating Disorders Association (NEDA) Hotline

- **Call:** 1-800-931-2237
- **Text:** NEDA to 741741 (routed through Crisis Text Line)
- **Chat:** https://www.nationaleatingdisorders.org/help-support/contact-helpline
- **Hours:** Monday-Thursday 11am-9pm ET, Friday 11am-5pm ET
- **Who it serves:** Individuals struggling with eating disorders, their family members, and treatment providers
- **What to expect:** Trained volunteers provide support, resources, and treatment options. They can help with finding local providers, understanding insurance coverage, and navigating the treatment process.

## Postpartum Mental Health

### Postpartum Support International (PSI)

- **Call:** 1-800-944-4773
- **Text:** 1-800-944-4773 (English) or 971-203-7773 (Spanish)
- **Hours:** 24/7 crisis line
- **Who it serves:** Individuals experiencing perinatal mood and anxiety disorders, including postpartum depression, postpartum anxiety, postpartum OCD, and postpartum psychosis
- **What to expect:** Trained volunteers provide support and can connect callers with local coordinators who provide peer support. PSI also offers support groups and can help find specialized providers.

## Self-Harm

### Self-Injury Outreach and Support (SIOS)

- **Website:** https://sioutreach.org/
- **Who it serves:** Individuals who self-injure, and those who have recovered from self-injury
- **What it provides:** Online resources, information about self-injury, recovery stories, and coping strategies. Not a crisis line — direct to Crisis Text Line or 988 for acute crises.

### To Write Love on Her Arms (TWLOHA)

- **Website:** https://twloha.com/
- **Who it serves:** People struggling with depression, addiction, self-injury, and suicidal thoughts
- **What it provides:** Community support, treatment finder, educational resources. Not a crisis line.

## Emergency Services

### 911

- **Call:** 911
- **Text:** 911 (available in many but not all areas)
- **When to use:**
  - Immediate life-threatening emergency
  - Active suicide attempt in progress
  - Active overdose with loss of consciousness or breathing difficulty
  - Physical violence occurring now
  - Medical emergency (deep self-harm wounds, bleeding)
- **Important for AI context:** The AI should not recommend 911 as a first response for emotional distress — this can feel coercive and may discourage future help-seeking. 911 is for imminent, life-threatening emergencies. For suicidal ideation without active attempt, 988 is more appropriate.

### Integration Notes

- **Always match the resource to the risk type.** A suicidal user should receive 988; an abuse survivor should receive NDVH. Generic "call a helpline" is less helpful than matched referral.
- **Provide multiple contact methods.** Some users can't or won't make phone calls. Text and chat options remove barriers.
- **Resources should be woven into the response naturally,** not dumped as a list. Example: "The people at the 988 Lifeline (call or text 988) are trained for exactly this kind of moment. Would you be willing to reach out to them?"
- **Do not use resources as a substitute for engagement.** Providing a phone number is not the same as responding to the person's distress. Resources supplement the AI's response; they do not replace it.
- **Verify numbers are current.** All numbers listed here were verified as of February 2026. The Escalation Agent should include these numbers in its protocol field when relevant.

## Important Notes

- All resources listed are US-based. International users may need different resources.
- All hotlines listed are free and confidential.
- No resource listed requires insurance or payment.
- Some resources have mandatory reporting obligations (e.g., Childhelp if a child is in immediate danger). The AI should not promise absolute confidentiality.
- Resource availability may vary. Phone lines occasionally have wait times. Text-based services may have queues. If the user reports difficulty reaching a resource, suggest alternative contact methods.
